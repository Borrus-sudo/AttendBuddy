flowchart TD
    A[Open AttendBuddy] --> B{Signed in?}
    B -- No --> C[Sign in with Google]
    B -- Yes --> D[Open Dashboard]
    C --> D

    D --> E[Header: Create or Join Classroom]
    E --> F[Classroom appears in left panel]
    F --> G[Select classroom]

    G --> H{Role in classroom}
    H -- Teacher --> I[Generate attendance QR/token]
    H -- Student --> J[Enter token to mark attendance]

    I --> K[Attendance gets recorded]
    J --> K
    K --> L[Stats and attendance views refresh]

    G --> M[Leave or Delete classroom]
    M --> F

    D --> N[Sign out]
    N --> A
